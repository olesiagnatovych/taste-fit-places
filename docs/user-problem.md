# The user problem

I am the first user. These are my own use cases, written down at the start of the project and kept close to how I described them.

## Who it is for

Someone who travels a lot, plans carefully and has specific taste. Not luxury taste: quality, atmosphere and real experience. Friends often ask me for recommendations because I find places none of them have heard of. That comes from research, not luck, and the research takes hours.

## What "good" means here

- **Quality over polish.** Genuinely tasty food and drink, not Michelin-style ceremony and not places built for photos.
- **Human atmosphere.** Staff and guests are themselves. Nobody is performing. Furniture can come from different sets; the place still has to be clean.
- **Niche over popular.** Natural wine with "funky" notes, light-roast specialty coffee, art with a real idea behind it. Many reviewers rate these things down, so the crowd's rating works against me.

## How I solved it before

1. Search Google Maps or social media for a category in an area.
2. Open place after place, scan photos and read reviews to guess whether it fits.
3. Cross-check against my own saved lists (many of them, by category).
4. Repeat for every trip, every neighborhood and every new category.

It works, but it costs hours. And it fails exactly when I have the least energy: tired after a long museum visit, hungry, in a city I don't know well, and still not willing to settle for a bad place.

## Use cases

- **"Natural wine near where I am."** Find wine bars that fit my wine taste and atmosphere. Tell me whether they have light bites only or a full kitchen, because which one I want depends on how hungry I am.
- **"Good coffee in a neighborhood I don't know."** Light or medium roast, specialty coffee, no dark roast.
- **"Art worth a detour."** Small galleries and installations with a real concept, not only the big museums.
- **"Somewhere to eat that won't disappoint."** Sushi or pizza that matches specific quality rules, within a sensible price range.
- **Location in my own words.** I often don't know exact neighborhood boundaries. "Lower East Side" may mean a larger area in my head than on the map, so a search can't depend on precise names.

## The two goals

1. **Time to value.** Get from "I want X near here" to a place that fits in minutes.
2. **Discovery.** Surface places that a normal search, sorted by rating and popularity, doesn't show.

## What is out of scope (for now)

- Planning a full day around opening hours, or a whole trip.
- Choosing a destination or an area to stay. I tested this and stopped (see decision 8 in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md)).
- Filters for who I travel with (family, friends, a dog). These are logged as future ideas.
- Other users' taste profiles (the cold-start problem). This is proposed, not built.
